Welcome to Congress High School

A tradition of excellence since 1998

For over two decades, Congress High School has combined rigorous academics with strong moral formation. Our motto, "Atya Omusana" — meaning "like the sun" — reflects our belief that every learner carries the potential to rise, shine and illuminate the world around them.

From modern science laboratories to a thriving co-curricular programme, we equip students with the knowledge, character and confidence to excel at UCE, UACE and beyond.
