Atya Omusana

Why "Like the Sun"?

Our motto, drawn from Luganda, translates to "how is the sun" — a reminder that the sun rises daily without fail, gives light unconditionally, and reaches everyone equally. We challenge every learner to embody these qualities: consistency, generosity of knowledge, and the ambition to shine brightly in whatever field they pursue.

Our Journey

School History

Key milestones that shaped Congress High School into what it is today.

1998

Foundation

Congress High School opens its doors with two streams of Senior One and a handful of dedicated teachers.

2006

First A-Level Class

The school introduces Advanced Level education, adding Science and Arts combinations.

2013

New Science Wing

A modern laboratory block is constructed, boosting practical learning capacity.

2019

ICT Hub Launched

Computer labs and digital learning resources are introduced school-wide.

2026

1,200+ Learners

Today, Congress High School serves over 1,200 students with an expanded boarding section.
