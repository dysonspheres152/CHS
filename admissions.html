Getting Started

Admission Requirements

General entry requirements for Senior One and transfer candidates.

📋

Senior One (S.1)

Original Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) result slip, birth certificate, and 2 passport photos.

🔁

Transfer Students

Transfer letter from previous school, latest report card, and a recommendation letter from the head teacher.

🎓

Senior Five (S.5)

Original UCE result slip meeting subject combination cut-off points, and a completed subject-choice form.

How It Works

Application Process

Four simple steps from application to admission.

1

Submit Application

Complete the online form below or collect a form from the school office.

2

Document Review

Our admissions office verifies your academic documents and results.

3

Interview / Interaction

Shortlisted candidates and parents attend a brief school interaction.

4

Confirm Admission

Pay the acceptance fee to secure your place before the term begins.
